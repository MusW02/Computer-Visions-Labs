================================================================
LAB TASK 3: REAL-TIME POSE ESTIMATION
================================================================

OBJECTIVE
---------
To detect human pose in real-time from a webcam feed using MediaPipe and visualize the skeletal structure and joint angles[cite: 273].

IMPLEMENTATION DETAILS
----------------------
1. Library Used: MediaPipe (Google's open-source framework) was used for pose estimation due to its high speed and accuracy on CPU[cite: 235].
2. Method: 
   - The webcam feed was captured using OpenCV[cite: 275].
   - Each frame was converted from BGR to RGB color space.
   - The `mp_pose.Pose` solution processed the frame to detect 33 3D landmarks on the human body[cite: 246, 230].
   - `mp_drawing.draw_landmarks` was used to overlay the skeleton connections[cite: 250].
3. Angle Calculation: 
   - Trigonometric calculations (arctan2) were applied to the coordinates of the Shoulder (12), Elbow (14), and Wrist (16) to calculate the elbow flexion angle in real-time[cite: 278].

OBSERVATIONS / EXPECTED OUTPUT
------------------------------
- The system successfully drew a "skeleton" overlay on the user's body in real-time[cite: 280].
- The landmarks (joints) tracked correctly even when the user moved fast.
- The calculated elbow angle updated dynamically (ranging from ~180° when the arm was straight to ~30° when fully flexed).
- Performance: The detection ran smoothly at high FPS on the CPU, confirming MediaPipe's efficiency for real-time applications compared to heavier models like OpenPose.
